# Results cited by the article

The runs behind the numbers of `blog/BLOG.md`, copied from the complete record of the
project (run numbers are those of the complete record, so they have gaps).
Each folder holds what `bench/record.sh` wrote for the run: `meta.txt`
(duration, validity, limits), `environment.txt`, `command.txt`, `summary.txt`,
`limits_*.txt`, `console.log` and, for driver runs, `driver/` with `summary.csv`
(percentiles per query and client count), `raw.csv` (every execution), `check.csv` (answer
hashes against the native reference), `queries/` and `plans/`. Account names, the home
folder and addresses are replaced; timings, answers and settings are as recorded. The chains that produced the
runs are in `bench/chains/`.
